1. Base Royalty System (Sales Volume)
Artist Monthly Sales	Royalty %
0–10 sales	30%
11–25 sales	35%
26–50 sales	40%
50+ sales	45%+ (Premier Artist)

This motivates them to actively promote their page to hit a higher bracket (like commission tiers in sales jobs).

2. Referral-Based Royalty Boost (Traffic Source Bonus)

If a customer buys because they clicked an artist’s link, that artist gets a bonus royalty percentage, because they helped acquire the customer.

Traffic Source	Artist Royalty Bonus
Direct store traffic (no referral)	Normal royalty (30–40%)
Customer came from Artist’s personal link (UTM tracking)	+5% bonus royalty
Artist brought in another artist who sells artwork	5% of referred artist’s royalties (lifetime or 12-month)

Example:

Print sells for $100

Print cost = $40

Base royalty = 35% ($35)

Came from artist’s link = +5% ($5 bonus)

Artist earns: $40 total

You still keep: $20 per sale → scalable with volume

3. Special Edition / High-Value Tier (Optional)

For limited-edition or high-ticket art:

Product Type	Artist Royalty
Standard Prints	30–40%
Limited Edition (numbered, signed, certificate)	40–50%
Artist collaborates with brand / influencer	Custom % (profit-share)
🧠 Why This System is Smart (Strategically & Financially)
Benefit to Artist	Benefit to You
They get paid more when they work harder or sell more	You don’t pay for ads — artists become your marketing team
They bring in their audience	You get new customers for free
Tiers create goals & competition	Artists stay motivated instead of becoming inactive
Referral artist % creates a mini “network effect”	Artists recruit more artists for you (for free)
Higher royalties = stronger loyalty	You become “the best paying platform” → faster artist signup → faster inventory growth
📊 Example with Real Numbers:

$120 art print sold via Artist’s link

Component	Amount
Sale price	$120
Printify cost	-$45
Artist base royalty (35%)	-$42
Referral traffic bonus (+5%)	-$6
Artist receives	$48
You earn	$27

You still keep 22% profit margin without spending on ads or content creation. That’s the genius.
