1000-Foot View — How Your Business Will Work (Simplified Flow) 1. Business Concept (Core Engine) You’re building an artist-powered print marketplace where: ✔ Artists upload their artwork → ✔ Artwork becomes print products on your Shopify store → ✔ Customer buys → printed on-demand (no inventory) → artist gets paid royalties. 2. Tool Ecosystem — Who Does What? Tool / Platform Purpose Shopify Your storefront (customers see this). Holds products, collections, checkout, payments, artist pages. Replit (Node.js app we’re building) Automation brain — receives CSV/art data → creates/updates products, collections, artists in Shopify automatically. PowerShell (optional/local) Lets you trigger Replit API from your computer easily. Tally Forms Artist submission form — artists upload bio, headshot, images → goes into your system. GPT-5 (me) Your strategist + coder + designer — building themes, writing code, generating roadmaps, fixing errors, remembering context like a partner. Printify/Printful (later-phase) On-demand printing and order fulfillment. You connect Shopify → Printify, so when someone orders, it prints & ships automatically. 🧠 Workflow Overview — From Artist to Customer 1️⃣ Artist Submits Art (Tally Form) Artist fills form with name, bio, headshot, and uploads their artwork. You or automation approve → art details saved (CSV, Google Sheet, JSON). 2️⃣ Automation Takes Over (Replit + PowerShell) You upload a CSV or hit a /run endpoint. Replit script automatically: ✅ Creates product in Shopify ✅ Sets template to "Art" ✅ Assigns artist metafields (bio, name, collection link, size, edition info) ✅ Creates “Artist Collection” if not already in store ✅ Assigns product to that collection 3️⃣ Shopify Displays it Nicely Homepage shows: Hero, Featured artists, Finishes, New drops. Product page shows: ✅ Artist bio section (“Meet the Artist”) ✅ “More from this Artist” gallery under product ✅ Limited edition badges (if applicable) 4️⃣ Customer Orders Shopify handles checkout Printify auto-fills and fulfills the order (when we connect that) You keep your margin, artist gets royalties. ✅ What You & I Have Already Accomplished ✔ Business branding (247 Print Network, artist-first concept) ✔ Custom homepage hero section + layout restored & improved ✔ Product templates (art template structure defined) ✔ JSON Homepage layout system rebuilt ✔ Liquid sections built for: ✅ "Meet the Artist" ✅ "More from This Artist" ✔ Replit automation server set up: ✅ API authentication ✅ Can update or create Shopify products from data ✅ Can assign metafields to products ✔ PowerShell runner that can call Replit automation ✔ Theme cleanup fixes (no more duplicated values, cleaned product pages) ✔ Manual → automated shift strategy in motion 🔜 What Needs to Be Finished (To Reach Launch) 🔥 Must-Do (Before Launch) Step Description 1.
